# Sangat Restaurant Multan: Research Report

Compiled 2 November 2026. Every fact below names its source. Anything not confirmed by at least one source is marked **UNVERIFIED** and is editable from the admin panel rather than hard-coded.

## How the research was done (and its limits)

Sources reached: Foodies Pakistan listing, Wanderlog (which mirrors Google Maps data), Yandex Maps, Mulk.pk, MenuPrices.pk, and web search results.
Sources that could not be read from this environment: Instagram (`@sangatforlife`) and TikTok (`@sangatrestaurant`) block automated reading, and Google Maps / Business Profile could not be opened directly. Foodies Pakistan and MenuPrices.pk publish the menu only as photos of the printed menu card, and those images could not be downloaded here, so **no prices could be verified**.

Verbatim review excerpts (Google, via Wanderlog):
- Zargham K, Jul 2023, 5★: "I enjoyed the place and its ambiance, especially during the winter season."
- Muhammad J, Nov 2024, 4★: "The Malai Boti and Kebabs were juicy and the Afghani Pulau was delicious."

- Wajahat J, Oct 2025, 4★: "It's nice to spend time here."

What guests like: ambience (especially winter evenings outdoors), BBQ (malai boti, kebabs), Afghani pulao, a place to spend time. What to improve: dessert quality (gulab jamun). Website implication: lead with the evening outdoor experience and BBQ; do not spotlight desserts.

The site seeds these reviews (with source and date) but owners should re-confirm before launch. The rating figure is admin-editable.
